# Community Guidelines

DevTrack contributors are expected to help maintain a respectful, constructive project space.

- Treat people with respect, including when you disagree.
- Critique ideas and code without personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language.
- Keep feedback relevant, actionable, and considerate.
- Respect privacy; do not publish another person's private information.

Project maintainers may edit, close, or remove contributions that violate these guidelines and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.
